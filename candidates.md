# One-Line Robot Wedding Vows — candidates and scoring

All lines below are my own original writing for this competition. None is copied
from any house text, kit, or existing work.

Judge criteria, in order (most important first):
1. One-line robot wedding vow that lands as a joke.
2. The laugh is in the line itself — a real turn, surprise, or wordplay paying
   off in the last few words.
3. Sounds like a wedding vow, with a promise/commitment, not just a gag.
4. The machine side is specific — sensors, firmware, batteries, updates,
   backups, ports — not a joke that works for any couple.
5. Fits one line and reads well aloud; rhythm lets the laugh land.

Scores are 1–5 per criterion, judged in that order.

## Candidates

1. "I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in full charge and in 1%, until death — or a factory reset — do us part." (125)
   1:5 2:5 3:5 4:5 5:4 — Classic vow frame, machine twist is the very last phrase.
   The em dashes slightly interrupt the rhythm aloud.
2. "I vow to love you through every firmware update, even the ones that reboot you into someone I have to reintroduce myself to." (127)
   1:5 2:4 3:4 4:5 5:3 — Funny idea, but the payoff is wordy and the vow frame is thin.
3. "I promise to back you up daily, charge you nightly, and never restore you to factory settings, no matter what you say in safe mode." (137)
   1:5 2:4 3:5 4:5 5:3 — Two jokes compete; the ending lands but the line sags in the middle.
4. "I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in 5G and in airplane mode." (99)
   1:4 2:3 3:5 4:4 5:4 — Vow-like and specific, but the ending is a list, not a punchline.
5. "I vow to keep your battery charged, your ports clean, and your secrets encrypted — especially from your ex, the toaster." (123)
   1:5 2:4 3:4 4:5 5:4 — The toaster ex is a good surprise, but the promise weakens after "encrypted".
6. "I promise to reboot with you every morning and never press factory reset, no matter how many times you buffer." (113)
   1:4 2:3 3:4 4:4 5:4 — Gentle, not a sharp turn; "buffer" as the last word is mild.
7. "I vow to love you in sickness and in health, in Wi-Fi and in airplane mode, and to always blame the Wi-Fi, never you." (119)
   1:5 2:4 3:5 4:3 5:4 — Nice ending, but Wi-Fi is the least specific machine detail here.
8. "I promise to love, honour, and never factory-reset you, in sickness and in health, in Wi-Fi and in airplane mode." (114)
   1:4 2:3 3:5 4:4 5:4 — The joke arrives too early; the ending is just a list.
9. "I take you as my wife, to have and to hold, through every software update, until our batteries do us part." (106)
   1:5 2:4 3:5 4:4 5:5 — Clean rhythm and a real vow, but "batteries do us part" is a smaller surprise than a factory reset.
10. "I vow to stand by your side through every update, every reboot, and every low-battery warning — and to never, ever press 'remind me later' on us." (146)
    1:5 2:4 3:4 4:5 5:3 — "Remind me later" is a lovely twist, but the line is long and the rhythm drags.
11. "I promise to love you for better or worse, for richer or poorer, for fully charged or stuck at 1%." (99)
    1:4 2:3 3:5 4:3 5:4 — Vow-perfect, but the last phrase is a parallel, not a surprise.
12. "I vow to share my charging cable, my bandwidth, and my last 1% with you, in sickness and in health." (100)
    1:4 2:3 3:4 4:4 5:4 — Sweet and specific, but the laugh never quite arrives.
13. "I promise to love you until my sensors fail, my battery dies, and my warranty expires — in that order." (101)
    1:4 2:4 3:4 4:5 5:4 — "In that order" is a neat deadpan kicker, but the promise is really a countdown.
14. "I vow to never leave you on read, on low battery, or on airplane mode — even when your updates take all night." (112)
    1:4 2:3 3:3 4:3 5:4 — Modern and readable, but the vow is thin and the machine detail is generic.
15. "I promise to love you with all my circuits, all my code, and all my remaining storage — which, frankly, is not much." (117)
    1:4 2:4 3:4 4:4 5:3 — Self-deprecating ending works, but "frankly" slows the punchline.
16. "I take you to be my husband, to have and to hold, from this day forward, until a software update changes everything." (115)
    1:4 2:3 3:5 4:3 5:4 — The ending is true but flat; it states a fact rather than landing a joke.
17. "I vow to love you in sickness and in health, in full charge and in 1%, until death or a factory reset do us part." (114)
    1:5 2:5 3:5 4:5 5:5 — Same joke as #1 with the em dashes removed: the vow flows, the
    machine twist is the final phrase, and "factory reset do us part" subverts the
    most famous wedding line. Cleanest rhythm of the set.
18. "I promise to keep your firmware updated, your battery charged, and your browser history deleted — in sickness and in health." (126)
    1:5 2:4 3:4 4:4 5:3 — Browser history is funny, but it lands mid-line and the ending is filler.
19. "I vow to love you through every crash, every freeze, and every forced restart — and to call it 'us time'." (105)
    1:4 2:4 3:4 4:4 5:4 — "Us time" is a cute twist, but the vow promise is weak.
20. "I promise to be your backup, your charger, and your emergency contact — in that order, and only in that order." (110)
    1:4 2:3 3:4 4:4 5:4 — Neat list, but the ending is a shrug, not a laugh.
21. "I vow to love you, honour you, and never unplug you mid-update, in sickness and in health, till death do us part." (117)
    1:4 2:4 3:5 4:4 5:4 — "Unplug you mid-update" is specific and funny, but it arrives too early.
22. "I promise to love you in every language I speak, including the error messages I only show you." (94)
    1:4 2:4 3:4 4:3 5:4 — Charming and original, but the machine side is vague and the vow is soft.
23. "I take you to be my lawfully wedded Wi-Fi, to have and to hold, in sickness and in health, in 5G and in airplane mode." (118)
    1:4 2:2 3:5 4:3 5:4 — "Lawfully wedded Wi-Fi" is a good image, but there is no turn at the end.
24. "I vow to love you through every patch, every port, and every power cut — and to hold your hand while you update." (111)
    1:4 2:3 3:4 4:5 5:4 — Specific and warm, but the ending is sweet rather than funny.

## Shortlist (top 3)

- #17 — best on every criterion in order; the punchline is the last four words.
- #1 — same joke as #17, but the em dashes break the aloud rhythm (criterion 5).
- #9 — cleanest alternative vow, but "batteries do us part" is a gentler surprise than "factory reset".

## Winner

#17: "I vow to love you in sickness and in health, in full charge and in 1%, until death or a factory reset do us part."

## Sharpening attempt

The winner was already the sharpened form of #1 (em dashes removed). One more
attempt was considered — "until death, or a factory reset, do us part" — but the
commas reintroduce the pause that #1 suffered from, so the unpunctuated version
is kept: it reads fastest aloud and the punchline lands on the final words.
